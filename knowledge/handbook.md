# UrbanRoof Pvt. Ltd. — Employee Handbook (Knowledge Base)

> Source: UrbanRoof Employee Handbook, approved 3 Sept 2022. This file is a cleaned,
> structured version for the HR assistant. Section titles are used as citations.
> Items marked ⚠️ AMBIGUITY are places where the handbook contradicts itself or is
> incomplete — the assistant must present both statements and direct the employee to HR.

## Working Hours & Attendance

- Ordinary work hours are **10:00 AM – 7:00 PM**.
- Clock-in window: **10:00–10:30 AM**. Clock-out window: **7:00–7:30 PM**.
- Reporting after the clock-in window counts as **late**. **More than 2 lates in a month
  causes a half-day salary deduction.** Exceptions need Reporting Manager approval.
- Employees may request flexible schedules within employer-defined limits, subject to
  Reporting Manager approval.
- If absent or arriving late/leaving early, notify your supervisor (and office manager)
  as far in advance as possible; in emergencies, as soon as possible.
- For absences longer than one day, telephone your immediate supervisor before the start
  of each scheduled workday. A doctor's certificate may be required for any
  illness-related absence.
- **Absence for 3 consecutive days without notifying** your supervisor is treated as
  voluntary termination of employment. Final settlement follows the FNF period of 90 days.
- ⚠️ AMBIGUITY (working week): the handbook states the normal work week is "54 hrs" in
  one place, defines a Full-Time Employee as working "at least 45 hours per week" in
  another, and grants holiday benefits to employees "who regularly work at least 35 hours
  per week" in a third. HR must confirm the official definition.

## Employment Types

- **Full-Time Employee**: regularly works at least 45 hours per week (⚠️ see ambiguity above).
- **Part-Time Employee**: works less than 45 but at least 17.5 hours per week.
- **Temporary Employee / Probation**: employed for a specific period of less than six
  months; entitled only to statutory benefits and what this handbook explicitly grants.

## Leave Policy — General Rules

- Leave applies to all **full-time (confirmed) employees**. No minimum service period is
  required for confirmed employees.
- **Management trainees and employees under probation get no paid leave** (see
  "Leave During Probation" for the limited exception).
- Leave requires approval from the Reporting Manager (48-hour turnaround expected).
  If the manager is unavailable, HR can directly approve **emergency leave only**.
- The handbook describes applying by email to the reporting manager with HR
  (hr@urbanroof.in) in loop. **Note: the company now uses HROne — apply through HROne
  and follow the handbook's timelines.** (⚠️ Handbook predates HROne; process wording
  is being updated.)
- **Late-communication penalty**: leave communicated after the deadline costs one
  additional leave (paid/unpaid) as penalty, and the sandwich rule may apply.
- **Sandwich rule**: if a week-off or holiday is clubbed with (falls adjacent to) your
  applied leave, the off day is also counted as a leave day.
- Verbally communicated leave is never valid — always apply in writing/HROne.

## Earned Leave (EL)

- **18 leaves per year**, credited **1.5 per month** (credited at the end of each quarter).
- Use for: casual leave, vacation, travelling outstation, official government work,
  personal work, family functions, other personal reasons.
- **Carry-forward: up to 7** leaves to the next year; EL above 7 lapses at year end.
  Carried-forward leave must be consumed within the next quarter.
- Application deadlines:
  - 0.5–1 day → at least **1 day** before the leave date
  - 2–4 days → at least **7 days** before
  - 5+ days → at least **1 month** before

## Sick Leave (SL)

- **12 leaves per year**, credited **1 per month** (credited at the end of each quarter).
- Use for: illness, hospitalization, accident/medical emergency, feeling unwell,
  other medical reasons.
- **No carry-forward** to the next year; must be consumed before year end.
- **3 or more days** of sick leave requires medical documents on the day of rejoining.
- Holidays and week-offs falling inside a sick-leave period are counted as leave days.
- Communication deadlines:
  - 0.5–1 day → phone/text + email at least **1 hour before shift**
  - 2 days → phone/text + email, same day of recommencement
  - 3+ days → email, same day of recommencement

## Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)

- Available when an employee worked on a week-off or holiday **at the request of the
  reporting manager** (self-initiated work on off days does not count).
- Email approval from the reporting manager is mandatory.
- **No carry-forward** to the next year.
- Application deadlines: 0.5–1 day → 1 day prior; 2–4 days → 7 days prior.

## Emergency Leave

- Covers **funeral leave (paid, up to 3 days)** for a death in the immediate family
  (spouse; own and spouse's parents; children; siblings; grandparents; grandchildren;
  sons/daughters-in-law), plus natural calamities, emergency facility closures, pandemic.
- Notify your manager or HR as soon as possible — any channel accepted (call, text, email).
- Emergency leave is not held against the employee.
- Consumption order: **SL first, then EL**; beyond available quota it becomes unpaid
  (loss of pay).

## Quarantine Leave

- **Unpaid.** Granted when an infectious disease in the employee's family/household makes
  attendance hazardous to others.
- Up to **30 days**, on the recommendation of an authorized medical attendant or public
  health officer. Medical documentation required.

## Vaccination Leave

- **3 paid leaves total**: 2 for the first vaccination dose, 1 for the second.
- Medical documentation must be submitted on rejoining.
- Apply at least 7 days before the leave date.

## Menstrual Leave (women employees)

- **12 paid leaves per year — 1 per month.** No carry-forward to the next month or year.
- The employee updates her cycle date with HR, hands over work to a colleague or the
  reporting manager before the leave, and delegates/postpones client meetings.
- Apply by email on the same day of recommencement.
- (The handbook spells this "Mensuration Leave" — it means menstrual leave.)

## Study / Sabbatical Leave

- **Unpaid.** For higher studies (or approved industry/consultancy engagements) with a
  guarantee of resuming the job afterwards.
- Apply by official email at least **2 months** before the leave.

## Maternity & Paternity Leave

- ⚠️ AMBIGUITY: the handbook lists Maternity Leave and Paternity Leave as leave types but
  **contains no rules for them** (no duration, eligibility, or process). Employees should
  contact HR directly; statutory maternity benefits under Indian law apply.

## Leave During Probation

- Employees under probation **cannot consume paid leave**.
- Exception: up to **2 leaves** during probation, only as sick / emergency / quarantine
  leave. ⚠️ AMBIGUITY: the handbook does not state clearly whether these 2 are paid or
  unpaid — confirm with HR.
- Probation leave deadlines: 0.5–1 day → 1 day prior by official email; up to 2 days →
  7 days prior by official email.

## Absenteeism

- Leave not informed within the specified timelines counts as **absenteeism**.
- 1st instance → verbal warning; 2nd → written warning; 3rd → strict action up to
  termination.
- Absence of **more than 7 working days** is treated as absconding; company facilities
  are suspended until further communication.

## Work From Home (WFH)

- Eligibility: role must be performable with basic hardware/software. **Employees on
  probation are not eligible.** Roles requiring site visits, on-site client meetings, or
  vendor coordination are not eligible unless the manager approves an exception.
- WFH can be occasional, temporary, or permanent. Accepted reasons include parenting,
  bad weather, emergencies, medical reasons, and an overlong commute.
- Manager checks before approving: role eligibility, cyber-security/data-privacy
  concerns, team collaboration impact, availability of equipment/software at home, and
  home working conditions (noise, internet, power).
- Expectations while on WFH: follow the work schedule, meet deadlines, submit **daily
  reports**, be online and reachable **9 hours** per working day, check in with your
  manager at least once a day, respond to colleagues/clients quickly.
- **Request procedure**: apply by email at least **15 days in advance** (unforeseen cases:
  as soon as possible). WFH longer than a week requires a goal-setting discussion with
  the manager and team.

## Dress Code

- Official dress code: **Formals / Business Casual**. Client-facing employees must wear
  formal or business casual.
- Always: clean and well-groomed; work-appropriate, clean, undamaged clothes; no
  offensive prints/slogans; grooming styles dictated by religion or ethnicity are not
  restricted.
- Formals (men): formal shirt and pants, formal shoes; blazer/suit optional.
  Formals (women): formal shirt with pants/skirt, formal western wear or modest
  traditional wear (saree/kurti), formal footwear.
- Business casual (men): jeans with polo/collared shirt. Business casual (women):
  pants/jeans with an appropriate top.
- Violations: supervisor/HR may reprimand or send the employee home to change; repeated
  or damaging violations can lead to stronger action up to termination.

## Salary & Payroll

- **Paychecks are distributed on the 7th of every month** (previous working day if the
  7th is a weekend or holiday). All salary deductions are itemized with the paycheck.
- Health insurance: company provides individual health insurance for eligible full-time
  and part-time employees from after the first full month of employment; the employee
  contributes **22% of the premium**.
- ⚠️ NOTE: the benefits section mentions US programs (Social Security/Medicare) and IRS
  mileage rates — these are template leftovers and do not apply in India. Ask HR about
  PF/ESI and current reimbursement rates.

## Expense Reimbursement

- Reimbursable: reasonable and necessary job expenses — travel, parking, business calls,
  required meals, conference/training fees and lodging (with prior written approval).
- **Receipts are mandatory** for every claimed expense. Forms are provided for claims and
  travel advances.
- Commute between home and office during normal hours is **not** reimbursable; transport
  for official work outside normal hours is covered.

## Performance Review

- Work is reviewed on an ongoing basis with the supervisor, plus a formal **annual
  performance review**. Both parties agree objectives for the coming year and sign the
  review form, which is kept in the personnel record.
- An employee may request a review of a personnel action: first discuss with the
  immediate supervisor, then with the CEO, whose decision is final.

## Resignation, Termination & Notice Period

- Either side may initiate separation.
- ⚠️ AMBIGUITY (notice period): one paragraph asks employees to give **at least four
  weeks (30 days) written notice**; the very next section says employees are encouraged
  to give **at least 10 business days** written notice. HR must confirm the binding
  notice period (check your appointment letter — a written employment agreement overrides
  the handbook).
- After notice, an exit interview is scheduled.
- Employees who resign or are terminated/laid off are entitled to accrued, unused
  vacation (earned leave) benefits.
- Final paycheck/settlement follows a **90-day FNF (Full & Final) period**.
- Grounds for discharge include (not limited to): falsifying records or application
  information, poor performance, insubordination, refusing reasonable overtime,
  negligence causing injury/damage, fighting, property damage, breach of
  confidentiality, conflicts of interest, misuse of company property, gambling on
  premises, dishonesty, theft, drugs/alcohol at work, weapons on premises, excessive
  tardiness/absenteeism, unauthorized absence, harassment or discrimination.

## Return of Company Property

- On separation (or on request), employees must return all company property: ID badges,
  keys, security passes, computers, storage media, credit/telephone cards, and all
  intellectual property/work products.
- Where law permits, the cost of unreturned property may be withheld from the final
  paycheck.

## Zero Tolerance Policy (ZTP)

Violations lead to strict action up to immediate termination (after investigation):

1. **Misuse of company assets** — using internal information or company assets for
   personal profit; secondary "for-profit" employment/moonlighting; misuse of budgets.
2. **Sexual harassment** — any offensive sexual behavior, comments, glances, or contact.
3. **Non-transparent transactions** — accepting gifts or favors in business dealings.
4. **Breach of confidential information** — trade secrets, IP, client-confidential data.
5. **Falsification of information** — misrepresentation or incomplete disclosure.
6. **Misbehavior at workplace** — alcohol/drugs/smoking in non-smoking areas; bullying,
   aggression, or causing injury; rule-breaking, loafing, negligence; vandalism, theft,
   hacking, damaging office property, misuse of gadgets/SIM/email; misuse of power,
   politicking, favoritism.

## Harassment & Equal Opportunity

- UrbanRoof provides equal employment opportunity without discrimination on race, color,
  religion, national origin, sex, age, marital status, sexual orientation, disability,
  political affiliation, appearance, or family responsibilities.
- Sexual harassment and discriminatory harassment are prohibited in all work-related
  settings, including trips and work social events.
- **Report harassment** to your supervisor or the CEO (if the complaint concerns the CEO,
  notify the designated grievance officer). Complaints are investigated promptly and as
  confidentially as practical. **No retaliation** against good-faith reporters or
  cooperating witnesses. Reasonable accommodations are available for employees with
  disabilities on request.

## Confidentiality

- Anything learned at work that is not public is confidential — including compensation
  data, program and financial information, donor/client information, and pending
  projects. Do not share it with anyone who doesn't need it for their work.
- Employees sign a non-disclosure agreement as a condition of employment. Disclosure
  leads to disciplinary action even if the employee gains nothing from it.
- Hold sensitive discussions only in private settings — not in lifts, restaurants, or on
  speakerphone.

## Computer, Internet & Information Security

- Company systems (PCs, email, voicemail, network) are company property and **may be
  monitored**; no expectation of privacy, even for items marked "private" or deleted.
- Do not: share passwords; bypass security; browse others' documents without business
  reason; change hardware/software configurations; install personal or unlicensed
  software; download programs from the internet without approval; use company systems
  for offensive content, solicitation, chain letters, gambling/pyramid schemes, personal
  commercial purposes, or political endorsements.
- Do: lock/turn off your PC when away, back up local files regularly, and assign
  appropriate confidentiality levels to documents.
- Internet access is provided for business purposes where approved; usage may be
  monitored and restricted.

## Solicitation & Distribution

- No soliciting (for memberships, money collection, political causes, etc.) on company
  premises during work time. Non-working areas/breaks are the exception for employees;
  outsiders may never solicit on premises.
- Posting or distributing literature requires prior approval from the CEO.

## Personnel Records

- Personnel files are company property, confidential, and access-restricted.
- Promptly notify your supervisor in writing of changes to personal data: address,
  phone, dependents, emergency contacts.
- Tampering with time records (yours or anyone's) is a disciplinary offence.

## Employment At Will / Contract

- Employment is "at-will" unless a written employment agreement signed by the CEO says
  otherwise. The handbook is not an employment contract and can be amended by the
  company at any time (except the at-will policy itself).
- ⚠️ NOTE: "at-will" is a US-law concept from the handbook template. In practice your
  appointment letter and Indian labour law govern termination terms — confirm specifics
  with HR.
